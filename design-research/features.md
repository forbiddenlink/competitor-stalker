# Category feature research

October 8, 2026. Nine direct/category leaders loaded in real Chromium, with homepage, product and supporting feature/pricing/tour pages captured. Similarweb was attempted and blocked. All nine accepted vendors were discovered through web search and checked on their own public websites.

Scope: inventory of capabilities publicly advertised on the loaded pages, including visible navigation and plan tables. This is not a verified account-level product audit: no signup, login, demo submission, paid purchase or integration occurred. Gated capabilities may exist beyond this inventory. Vendor promises are vendor claims, not independently proven results. Pricing numbers, quotas and testimonial outcomes are deliberately not transplanted into this site.

## Candidate gaps, ranked for this site's actual constraints

Final implementation scope will be fixed in phase 3. These are evidence-led candidates, not promises of connected services.

| Priority | Gap and peer precedent | Fit and safe implementation boundary |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Actionable briefing, review queue and research freshness — Crayon, Contify, Kompyte | Use existing records, update dates and snapshots; link straight to analysis. Label saved/local activity accurately. Never label all edits verified research. |
| 2 | Competitor find/filter/sort and useful selection — Visualping, Rival IQ | Derive controls from current competitor fields; consistent keyboard/touch handling. No storage schema required. |
| 3 | Local change feed / before-after comparisons — Visualping, Hexowatch, Competitors App | Reuse existing snapshots. Disclose history covers local saved changes, not live market events. Keep alerts' existing content/features. |
| 4 | Evidence-aware battlecard summary — Klue, Crayon, Kompyte | Compose current competitor, feature, SWOT and strategy fields; identify missing inputs. Copy/print report without AI-generated facts. |
| 5 | Comparison controls and accessible data views — Rival IQ, Brandwatch | Sort/filter existing pricing/matrix records; keyboard alternatives to plotting; readable mobile tables and legends. |
| 6 | Social research workflow — Competitors App, Rival IQ | Open appropriate public research links and explain manual figures/scanning limits. Preserve existing actions; no fake ingestion. |
| 7 | Exportable brief/report — Crayon, Rival IQ, Competitors App | Add browser print and readable text/CSV where current data supports it. Preserve existing JSON export/import semantics. |
| 8 | Guided empty states and next actions — across leaders | Real add/import/profile actions, visible validation/success/error states, no invented testimonials or commercial outcomes. |
| needs-approval | Source/provenance fields, review dates and evidence confidence | Persisting new content structure needs owner approval under the CMS/section constraint; derive existing URL/update metadata in this upgrade. |
| needs-approval | Automated crawls, AI, email/Slack digests, scheduled social/news/SEO feeds | External services, jobs, credentials, paid usage and delivery commitments. Defer. |
| needs-approval | Shared accounts, permissions, CRM, win/loss pipeline and cloud sync | Backend and new content models; does not fit the current purely local architecture. Defer. |

The high-impact lesson is to complete the research → comparison → decision loop with existing intelligence. Breadth of enterprise vendor offerings does not justify adding disconnected dashboards, unverified numbers or simulated automation.
