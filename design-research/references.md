# Live design references

Researched October 8, 2026. Thirteen accepted live references, including five clearly outside competitive-intelligence/workflow software: publishing, investment, venture capital, drone inspection and a creative agency. This is a transferable design collection for an analytical workspace, not a claim that every reference is a direct competitor.

Every accepted target was opened in Chromium at 1440×1000 and captured at the top, middle and bottom. The images show the live site on this date; gallery screenshots are discovery evidence only. Public pages were inspected, not authenticated products. Native screenshots are preserved individually; contact sheets are resized review aids.

## Gallery provenance and limitations

- **Awwwards:** the Site of the Day index and TWKS/Lidar award-detail pages loaded in Chromium. The details identify SOTD awards on October 7 and October 6, 2026 respectively. Initial live captures showed preloaders; accepted captures use a 20-second settling period with normal motion. Edolus is excluded after its retry failed.
- **SiteInspire:** app/business category pages and the individual Retool, Plain, Fey and Stripe Press entries loaded in Chromium. Current live designs can differ from the submitted gallery images.
- **Godly:** `godly.website` redirects to **Recent Design**. Its live website collection loaded and linked Mintlify, Topology, Paste and AuthKit. This provenance is explicitly the current redirect, not an invented surviving Godly gallery.
- **Land-book:** both the homepage and tech gallery were blocked by Cloudflare (403) in Chromium. Web search/text access exposed the linked Time.fyi entry, Capsule listing and Paper in the Weave entry's related recommendations. All three target sites independently loaded in Chromium. Gallery provenance for these is text discovery, not visual inspection of a protected gallery.

Evidence: `gallery-targets-evidence.json`, `gallery-details-targets-evidence.json`, `awwwards-links-targets-evidence.json`, `winner-details-targets-evidence.json`. Each records URLs, final URLs, HTTP responses, text and links. The invalid Awwwards technology-category URL returned 404; it is a failed discovery path, not evidence that Awwwards is inaccessible.

## Findings to carry into phase 3

Build an authored intelligence workspace: readable report typography, precise small metadata, fine rules, differentiated section density and a distinct primary action. Paper, Stripe Press and Mintlify give the strongest foundation; Retool and Paste demonstrate how to make real tool content understandable. The unrelated industries supply composition and restraint, not their functionality. Heavy entrance animations, low-contrast showcase text and decorative product mockups should not enter essential user journeys.

Do not make this dashboard a marketing landing page. The primary work remains researching competitors, comparing evidence and deciding what to do next. Final tokens, features and page-by-page decisions belong in phase 3.
