# Phase 2 decisions and validation

Research precedes visual implementation so the upgrade can be justified against actual sites and user workflows. The collection combines product clarity with editorial structure rather than copying a single marketing homepage into a dashboard.

Accepted thirteen references only after native Chromium captures showed finished content. Replaced blank Shopify Design and failed Edolus attempts with Paste and AuthKit. Kept all failed evidence. Kept Fey only as a current minimalist reading reference, explicitly documenting its closed signups and changed business state. Land-book's protected galleries are marked blocked; independently loaded target sites remain usable references with qualified discovery provenance.

Nine competitors provide sufficient category coverage: direct intelligence tools, change monitors and social intelligence/reporting leaders. Home, product and supporting public pages reveal capabilities without creating accounts, submitting forms or purchasing access. Similarweb's 403 does not support a feature inventory. Public marketing claims are not hands-on verification.

Implementation candidates prioritize work the app can perform using existing local data: find intelligence, understand saved changes, compare competitors and produce a useful decision brief. Persisted new fields, external monitoring, outbound delivery, cloud/CRM accounts and paid data remain deferred in `needs-approval.md`.

Validation: thirteen accepted design sites with three captures each; twenty-seven loaded peer pages with three captures each; blocked attempts retained; all screenshot links in the two research documents resolve; research capture script passes Node syntax checking; tracked documentation diff passes whitespace checking. No application source or dependencies changed, so application tests/build/Lighthouse are still pending in the implementation verification phase.

This phase commits research and the continuation checkpoint only, on `design/upgrade`. No merge, push or deployment.
